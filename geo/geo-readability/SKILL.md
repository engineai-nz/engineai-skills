# Geo Readability

Assess and improve content readability for both humans and AI parsers.

## Process

1. Analyze current reading level
2. Check sentence length and complexity
3. Evaluate paragraph structure
4. Assess heading hierarchy
5. Review formatting and scannability
6. Provide specific rewrite suggestions

## Output

- Readability score and analysis
- Specific sentences/paragraphs to rewrite
- Structural recommendations
- Before/after examples
