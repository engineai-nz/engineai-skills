# Geo Keyword Research

Find keywords worth targeting for generative engine visibility.

## Process

1. Understand the business, audience, and goals
2. Identify seed topics and questions
3. Expand into related keywords and phrases
4. Analyze search intent for each keyword
5. Prioritize by relevance, volume potential, and competition
6. Map keywords to content types

## Output

- Prioritized keyword list with intent classification
- Content type mapping (blog, landing page, FAQ, comparison)
- Question-based keywords (People Also Ask, voice search)
- Competitor keyword gaps
